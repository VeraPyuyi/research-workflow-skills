# Independent Review and Disagreement Resolution

Use this mode for substantive review conflicts, central mathematical or scientific
claims, or a requested multi-model council. Keep routine checks proportional.
Use the shared model map and current resource allowance; a council is optional,
not a reason to invoke every available model.
This is internal adjudication, not the blind venue mode. Queue ordinary findings
at the applicable milestone; use [venue-review.md](venue-review.md) for M4/M5
without exposing this adjudication history to either fresh reviewer.

## Independent evidence before synthesis

Assign one bounded scientific question to each appropriate reviewer with the same
artifact revision, original sources, definitions, data and relevant constraints.
Let reviewers first extract or solve independently. Avoid sharing the author's
desired verdict or another review's conclusion in that first pass.
For cross-review, anonymize model names when useful while retaining provenance
privately and preserving every source/assumption needed for assessment.

Then exchange specific disagreements and their witnesses. A single integrator
reconciles them against the original artifacts. Keep a minority objection when it
has unresolved evidence; neither a majority, model prestige nor the integrator's
confidence establishes truth. If tools or independent models are unavailable,
deliver the actual partial/self-review and an unexecuted review package.

## Adjudicate findings before changing artifacts

Each finding has an identity, location and revision, observation, supporting
evidence, scientific consequence, requested correction and destination stage.
Check that the reviewer received the complete intended artifact. A missing page
or truncated equation can explain an apparent defect.

Verify an incoming finding against the actual source and, where useful, a small
calculation or counterexample. Check definitions and standing assumptions outside
the cited sentence. Record one disposition:

- Confirmed: the claimed defect is demonstrated within scope.
- Refuted: the actual source or valid calculation contradicts the objection.
- Narrowed: there is a problem, but it affects a smaller claim or has lower impact.
- Unresolved: required evidence is absent or the disagreement remains open.

These are review dispositions, not new claim-evidence or proof states.
An out-of-domain example is not a counterexample. An expression undefined without
an integrability condition is not automatically a false inequality. Inspect the
proposed fix separately: it may remove a needed assumption, introduce a new claim,
delete unique evidence or weaken correct work unnecessarily.

## Repair, recheck and stop

Route confirmed changes to the responsible stage under existing authorization.
Record old/new artifact revisions and the evidence that each repair addresses.
Recheck the repaired location plus affected dependent claims, figures, proofs,
code or tests. Expand to a full review when a core definition/protocol change
invalidates earlier coverage; otherwise preserve valid prior checks.

Close an issue only with a checked resolution, not the author's "fixed" message.
Keep unresolved disagreements and user-owned scientific decisions visible.
Stop the review loop at its agreed scope/resource boundary or when the
confirmation pass finds no remaining material issue within inspected coverage.
Do not iterate until models agree or a desired score appears.
