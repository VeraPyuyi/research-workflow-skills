# Review Scientific Claims and Evidence

Read the [shared contract](../research-navigate/references/workflow-contract.md)
and applicable [domain references](../research-navigate/references/domains/index.md).
Read [model routing](../research-navigate/references/model-routing.md) to assign
independent review branches, and
[reuse and simplification](../research-navigate/references/reuse-and-simplification.md)
when the artifact may contain duplicated theory, implementation, evidence or
unnecessary experiments.
For conflicting reviews or substantive council work, read
[independent review](references/independent-review.md). Verify incoming objections
and proposed fixes against original artifacts before applying them; after repair,
recheck changed locations and affected dependencies, preserving valid prior checks.
At a genuine candidate choice - which disposition, repair route or reuse decision
to adopt - read
[evidence comparison and optional Jev](../research-navigate/references/decision-support.md).
Use inspected evidence and program constraints first, a bounded native comparison
when useful, then the integrator decision. Comparison has no proof or gate authority.

## Choose the review mode

Internal evidence audit may inspect project records and repair history.
At the applicable milestone, use [research-focus](../research-focus/SKILL.md)
to compare the original theme, cumulative pivots and actual headline contribution.
Check whether an auxiliary result has replaced an important question, while
allowing evidence-backed in-theme negative findings and changed methods.
For a target-venue simulation, read [venue review](references/venue-review.md):
launch a new agent with `fork_context: false` and only the frozen submitted paper,
permitted attachments and verified venue criteria. Do not give it this project's
history, author self-assessment or previous reviews. Full-paper work uses two
scored rounds at M4 and M5 (every final reviewer must give at least 8/10 for paper completion), with distinct reviewer model families and fresh agents across all rounds, panel slots and retries; an explicitly bounded
review remains one review. Context independence, model independence and filesystem
isolation are different facts and must be reported separately.
Private focus anchors, user-intent history and alignment verdicts remain with the
author-side coordinator. Blind reviewers reconstruct the question from the paper;
they do not receive the intended answer or a directive to preserve the author's thesis.

## Review the artifact actually supplied

Before M4/M5 reviewer admission and final delivery, apply the
[submission leakage audit](references/submission-leakage.md) to the actual sources,
rendered paper and every submitted attachment, including nested archives and
metadata. Remove local/private paths, credentials and accidental internal agent
content from submission copies; explicit infrastructure placeholders are allowed.
Preserve legitimate scientific AI discussion and required AI-use declarations.
Record unresolved findings and uninspected surfaces; neither a filename allowlist
nor an automated scan alone establishes a clean submission. Keep this author-side
audit outside blind-review inputs and reuse valid coverage between milestones.

Use an independent context/reviewer when available and authorized within the task;
provide the question, artifacts and relevant evidence without steering toward a
desired verdict. If performing self-review, identify that limitation. AI review
does not constitute actual external peer review.

1. Reconstruct the central claims and the evidence needed to establish them.
   Check closest-work distinctions against inspected sources and search coverage.
2. Examine question-to-design alignment, measurement validity, controls, data/split
   leakage, resource fairness, assumptions and identification where applicable.
3. Reconcile results with raw/aggregated records, selection/exclusion rules,
   uncertainty and failure reporting. Distinguish an invalid conclusion from a
   narrower supported statement.
4. Check consistency, citation support, reproducibility details and practical
   limitations. Do not demand unrelated experiments merely to lengthen a review.
   For a presentation-focused review, verify that the claimed question, comparison,
   evidence, denominator, display and bounded conclusion form one chain. Check
   caption/body division and final-size readability, but do not confuse polished
   prose or design with scientific support.
5. Audit whether prior theory, methods, benchmarks or implementations can replace,
   shorten or merge the current content. Check assumptions, quantifiers, proof or
   implementation equivalence, and whether removing an experiment would remove
   evidence the reused result does not provide. Treat a plausible simplification
   as unverified until its conditions are checked.
6. Calibrate findings by effect: critical invalidates a central claim or reported
   evidence; major requires substantive revision/evidence; minor improves clarity
   without changing the scientific conclusion.

For theoretical claims, inspect the actual definitions, quantifiers, assumptions,
source conditions and proof dependencies. Check for circular/vacuous assumptions,
missing steps, invalid limit/expectation operations and relevant counterexamples.
Distinguish a sound conditional result from unsupported transfer to the application.
Report which statements and steps were checked and which remain unverified;
author derivation, independent AI review and machine verification are distinct.
Neither successful simulations nor the label "supported" certifies a theorem.
An example outside the assumptions, or an expression that becomes undefined,
does not by itself refute a theorem. Record the missing condition precisely.

Use native scientific and mathematical reviewers for ordinary substantive checks and
an independent long-evidence reviewer for manuscript-wide claims. Escalate a
disputed central result to a stronger model rather than accepting model consensus.
State the reviewer's identity and independence. Review the actual derivation,
source, code, data, run record or manuscript, not only the author's summary.
These internal-audit inputs do not override the blind mode's submitted-input limit;
missing necessary evidence in a submission is a finding, not permission to browse
the author's private records.

## Deliver and finish

Deliver strengths and actionable findings, each with location, observed evidence, scientific
impact, requested correction and destination stage. Separate required fixes from
optional extensions. If nothing material is found, state what was inspected and
what remains unverified.

For reuse findings, distinguish "replace current work", "needs an equivalence
proof", "analogy only" and "retain current work". Include the effect on novelty,
proof status, experiment necessity and paper claims.

Use survey/ideate for contribution gaps, design for validity/identification,
baseline/implement for comparison correctness, experiment/analyze for missing
evidence, and write for unsupported wording. Add the shared handoff, reviewer and
review-coverage record, and readiness assessment; an AI assessment does not replace
the user's conclusion decision.

Consolidate internal reviews at the research/protocol gate and
principal-conclusions gate; for full papers use M4/M5 venue reviews and the
submission/release gate's packaging checks. M2 holds engineering checks. Use
existing valid coverage; do not launch a complete review for each edit or handoff.
Reopen only affected coverage after a substantive change. Do not call a required
gate passed when its branch is unexecuted or its evidence is inaccessible.
In-scope conclusions may use explicit startup delegation; otherwise preserve the
user's conclusion decision. Submission and release always retain their authority.

Example: when "equal cost" contradicts the reported budget, correct the claim or
design a genuinely comparable study. Do not treat a textual edit as new evidence.

## Mathematical work when applicable

At the applicable milestone, check theorem imports, quantifiers, common witnesses and the connection to actual upstream inputs and the original conclusion. Separate local proof validity from applicability. This adds no per-lemma review gate.
Read [mathematical work](../research-navigate/references/mathematical-work.md) for the optional record and return contract.
