# Survey and Read Research

Read the [shared contract](../research-navigate/references/workflow-contract.md)
and applicable [domain references](../research-navigate/references/domains/index.md).
Read [model routing](../research-navigate/references/model-routing.md) to assign
native search and synthesis branches and milestone evidence review.
At a genuine candidate choice - which transfer record, closest-work reading or
reuse decision to pursue - read
[evidence comparison and optional Jev](../research-navigate/references/decision-support.md).
Use inspected evidence and program constraints first, a bounded native comparison
when useful, then the integrator decision. Comparison has no proof or gate authority.
Read [reuse and simplification](../research-navigate/references/reuse-and-simplification.md)
when prior theory, methods, benchmarks or implementations may replace or shorten
current work.
For claim-bearing synthesis, use the
[evidence ledger](../research-navigate/references/evidence-ledger.md) to preserve
source identity, actual inspected locations and unresolved gaps through writing.

## Choose the requested mode

- **Close reading:** inspect the supplied paper and necessary supporting material;
  do not expand into a full field survey unless the task needs it.
- **Search/update:** define question, concepts, inclusion criteria and search
  coverage; search suitable scholarly/primary sources and record queries/date.
- **Synthesis:** compare verified source records by question, assumptions,
  mechanism, evidence, conditions, limits and unresolved disagreement.

For open topic exploration, theory building, unexplained mechanisms or a stalled
research direction, consider connections beyond the immediate field. Read
[cross-domain survey](references/cross-domain-survey.md) for this mode. Search by
problem structure as well as keywords, including relevant foundational mathematics.
Do not turn a bounded paper reading into an exhaustive interdisciplinary survey.

For a substantive survey, split independent search, full-text extraction and
source-check branches when suitable. Use native retrieval and synthesis executors
from the model map; at the applicable milestone have a different model check the evidence map, assumptions and
closest-work claims. Record searches or branches that did not run.

At entry, resumption or a handoff, a compatible validated suite release may be
adopted under recorded authority through
[workflow updates](../research-navigate/references/workflow-updates.md); running
jobs keep their pinned versions and customized local copies stay untouched.

Verify title/authors/source/version and accessible content. Inspect original
papers or artifacts for the claims being used. Record whether full text, selected
sections, abstract or metadata were actually read. If access or browsing is
unavailable, analyze the supplied material and mark verification gaps.

Separate what authors report from what the evidence establishes and from your
interpretation. For each important finding, record the supporting location,
setting, controls and limitations. Distinguish different versions of the same
work; do not count them as independent confirmation.
Check bibliographic identity and support for the attributed claim separately.
For a disputed key claim, ask an independent source question before supplying the
preferred interpretation; then reconcile the answer with the draft. A failed
retrieval does not establish fabrication.

Compare closest work on the actual proposed contribution, not only title keywords.
Mark a gap as "not found in this search" when coverage is limited. Refresh relevant
work at topic selection and submission preparation when current novelty matters.

Look for prior results that can simplify the project as well as gaps that justify
new work. For each candidate, record whether it is directly reusable, needs an
argument, requires a model change, is analogy only or cannot replace the current
content. Do not infer novelty from failure to find a keyword match.

## Deliver and finish

For a paper reading, deliver its identity/access status, research question,
supported method/evidence summary, critical limitations and follow-up questions.
For a survey, add a source index, comparison/evidence map, disputed findings,
closest work, search coverage and reusable code/data when verified.
In cross-domain mode, include prioritized transfer records: source assumptions and
result, target correspondence, unmet conditions, applicability versus analogy,
possible insight and the smallest useful check. Unretrieved leads stay unverified.
When a prior result can replace current theory, code or experiments, deliver a
reuse audit record with its conditions, expected simplification and contribution
impact. Route unverified equivalences to ideate/design and review, not directly to
deletion.

Finish with the shared handoff, routing research opportunities to ideate or
missing methodological evidence back to targeted reading. Do not fabricate a
citation, experimental detail or peer-review status.

Example: an abstract reporting faster training supports a source-reported speed
claim, not an inferred hardware setup, confidence interval or generalization gain.
