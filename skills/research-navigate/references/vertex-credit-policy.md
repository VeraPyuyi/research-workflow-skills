# Vertex: App Builder promotional credit only

The authorized funding source is **Trial credit for GenAI App Builder**.
Uncovered usage, paid overage, automatic top-up and other paid channels are not
authorized. A model name, successful response, billing-enabled project or generic
`allowance_coverage: "confirmed"` does not establish this credit's coverage.

## Evidence required before a reviewed re-enable

Use the official account's Billing > Credits entry, its promotion terms and,
where already available, SKU-level cost/credit reports. Record only local
references to account details:

- Exact credit identity and active status, remaining value, currency, start/end
  times and the observation time.
- Billing account/project correspondence and the credit's service/SKU scope.
- The actual API, model/version, region and all chargeable features mapped to
  covered SKUs. Include grounding, caching, storage or auxiliary calls when used.
- Shared consumption and in-flight reservations, plus a conservative request
  bound within the remaining credit.
- A verified way to prevent uncovered charges and exhausted-credit overage.
  Budget alerts or a mixed group's fallback order are not spending enforcement.

If the terms cover only a search or conversational-agent service, investigate
that service's supported interface separately. Renaming a Gemini endpoint does
not change its billing scope. Do not probe eligibility with inference calls,
guess balance APIs, upgrade billing or add a new service to consume the credit.

Re-enable only the verified subset after updating the adapter, evidence and
admission tests. A later catalogue refresh or compatible skills upgrade must not
silently restore the old Vertex assignments. Unknown/expired coverage stays on
hold while independent work continues.
