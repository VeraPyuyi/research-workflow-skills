# Frozen Study Protocol

The detailed frozen protocol is retained with the workflow's internal validation
record. This release copy states the scientific scope and limitations without
private paths or raw source text.

## Sampling

For each venue-year, four officially selected papers and two ordinary accepted
controls were selected before qualitative reading using official metadata,
research-type/domain tags and a fixed SHA-256 ordering. Selection sought method,
theory, empirical and benchmark coverage but did not replace papers for writing
quality. Comparators are purposive and not tightly matched; this is not a random
sample of all conference papers.

## Split

Within each six-paper cell, slots 1, 2, 3 and 5 enter discovery; slots 4 and 6
enter holdout. A planning-pilot paper was moved out of holdout and exchanged with
an unread selected peer in the same cell before discovery reading. The exchange
and preliminary availability manifest are recorded in the private implementation
record. No holdout paper was read before the pattern cards were frozen.

## Reading

Readers read the full main text, confirmed body boundaries, inspected necessary
appendix material and viewed actual detail pages for figures/tables. They recorded
PDF-page locators, identity checks, extraction limitations and exact coverage.
Overview thumbnails were orientation aids only; detail images established visual
observations. Machine text extraction was not treated as human reading.

## Pattern rules

Observations are labeled single-paper, repeated or editorial recommendation.
A cross-venue repeated pattern needs at least three independent papers and two
venues. Holdouts are evaluated separately for transfer and counterexamples.
Frequency is descriptive; selected status is not an outcome variable for causal
claims and does not predict acceptance.

## Validation

The four synthetic behavior fixtures were frozen before instruction changes.
Old-skill outputs were produced in a fresh context without the rubric or expected
answers. New outputs use the same raw inputs and are judged for incremental
benefit. Figures/tables require actual execution; renderer checks include
denominators, missing latency, failures, safe provenance, reproducibility and
visual readability at the target width. Checks are consolidated at the final
milestone rather than after every edit.
