# Write an Evidence-Linked Paper

Read the [shared contract](../research-navigate/references/workflow-contract.md)
and applicable [domain references](../research-navigate/references/domains/index.md).
Read [model routing](../research-navigate/references/model-routing.md) for free
drafting branches and independent review, and
[reuse and simplification](../research-navigate/references/reuse-and-simplification.md)
when presenting prior results or simplified content.
Use [the evidence ledger](../research-navigate/references/evidence-ledger.md) for
substantive claims and numerical statements.
For a full manuscript, major section or paper-facing figure argument, read the
[presentation guide](references/paper-presentation.md). Select the applicable
method, theory, empirical or benchmark spine and form an argument outline before
drafting. Use its calibrated language and display guidance; do not copy sample
sentences or infer that a conference category causes acceptance.

## Build from available evidence

Write submission-facing content separately from internal project records. Use
relative paths or explicit infrastructure placeholders, never real machine paths,
credentials, private endpoints, chat fragments or execution notes in the paper.
Preserve truthful scientific AI descriptions and required AI-use declarations.
Before freezing a complete/revised manuscript, apply the
[submission leakage audit](../research-review/references/submission-leakage.md)
to sources, rendered pages, metadata and intended attachments. Keep audit records
private; reuse unchanged coverage and recheck only repaired surfaces.

Use the [research-focus](../research-focus/SKILL.md) theme/current-thesis reminder
to organize title, abstract and headline contributions. Auxiliary theory,
engineering and calibration stay in their justified roles. Assess whole-paper
alignment at M4/M5 author-side; exclude private anchors/history from blind inputs.
Do not strengthen claims merely to make them match the intended storyline.

For a contribution-improvement round, consume its contribution audit and
claim-evidence map with the approval record. Carry the round identity into the
revision handoff. Use observation, explanation, conditions and meaning to improve
the argument while distinguishing approved findings from unverified hypotheses.
Retain necessary context and unfavorable findings; improved wording is not new
evidence. Return changed claim locations and remaining gaps to the same round.

For theory materials, retain definition/assumption and source versions. Distinguish
an inspected source result, an author-derived argument awaiting checking, a checked
proof, a conjecture, an analogy and an empirical observation. Match every theorem
statement to its actual proof and scope; mark gaps explicitly. A direct application
is not automatically a new theorem, and experiments do not supply missing proofs.

For reused theory, methods or implementations, state the source and condition under
which the result applies. Distinguish prior work, project adaptation and project
addition. Do not remove a necessary experiment from the argument merely because a
related prior theorem exists; retain the evidence that supports implementation,
generalization, mechanism or real-task claims.

1. Inspect the brief, sources, protocol, analysis/claim map, existing manuscript,
   review comments and approved conclusions. Preserve the chosen writing tool,
   language and format; otherwise propose a suitable representation.
2. Organize the argument around the question, closest work, contribution, method,
   evidence, limits and reproducibility. Keep contribution wording within its
   supported scope. Give each section a reader question and role in the evidence
   chain, not just a topic label.
3. Write framing and method records early when useful. If experiments or main
   conclusion review are pending, explicitly mark result slots and evidence gaps.
   A hypothesis or outline note is not a measured result.
4. Build figures/tables from recorded data and transformations. Link each number
   to run/data versions and aggregation. State the reader question, comparison,
   denominator and uncertainty before choosing an encoding; keep caption and
   body complementary. Use research-analyze for the evidence specification and
   research-implement for generated outputs when appropriate. Reuse verified
   citations; inspect primary material for claims not yet supported by the
   source record.
5. Report relevant assumptions, controls, uncertainty, failure/negative findings,
   resource conditions, data/code access and reproducibility limits. Separate
   real measurements, simulations and proposed work.
6. At the complete-manuscript milestone, check consistency across abstract, text,
   figures, appendix and references. Routine edits receive only needed local
   checks; do not trigger a full citation sweep or manuscript review each time.
   Use appropriate artifact tooling when layout or compilation matters and record
   actual validation rather than claiming an unperformed render/build.
   Preserve the type and level of every uncertainty estimate; an untyped `+/-`
   is not automatically a confidence interval. Keep unresolved source or result
   gaps visible with a concrete next check. A metric/source change invalidates
   the relevant prior review and dependent text until reconciled.

## Deliver and finish

Deliver the actual manuscript or bounded requested section, figure/table provenance,
references, appendix/reproducibility material, and explicit evidence gaps.
Use the shared handoff. Scientific challenges route to research-review; missing
evidence routes to survey, design or experiment/analyze.
For full-paper delivery, read
[Goal execution](../research-navigate/references/goal-execution.md). Route the
complete submitted bundle to M4 venue review, address findings, then send the
revised bundle to a different fresh agent at M5. Neither blind reviewer receives
internal evidence, optimizer records, Jev comparisons or prior comments.
Keep raw reports and author-side dispositions
in internal delivery records, not automatically in public submission attachments.
After round 2, make scoped repairs/rechecks and prepare final venue-specific paper
and reproduction materials with research-submit. A section or outline is not the
full-paper Goal's endpoint.

Manuscript readiness and submission authorization are separate. Do not invent
author information, acknowledgments, completed experiments or venue requirements.

Example: an efficiency hypothesis can appear in the motivation and protocol while
the Results section remains explicitly pending measurements. This lets writing
advance without upgrading an expectation into a finding.
