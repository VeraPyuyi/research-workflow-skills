# NeurIPS discovery synthesis

This bounded presentation study covers twelve discovery papers: nine selected papers and three controls. Comparisons are purposive and genre-sensitive, not estimates of an award effect. Acceptance category guarantees neither clarity nor scientific validity. The strongest recurring presentation pattern is a traceable connection between the motivating question, the proposed mechanism, the displayed evidence, and the stated limits.

Several papers make that connection through explicit navigation. Unpaired Multi-Domain Causal Representation Learning separates joint-distribution recovery from graph recovery, then distinguishes identifiability from finite-sample illustration (pp2,6-10). CAN moves from theoretical properties to a two-stage procedure and empirical questions (pp3-10). Goldilocks uses everyday dialogue before operational scoring and five insight headings (pp1-9). The GenZ control likewise organizes evaluation around questions and distinguishes task progress from safety (pp7-10). These devices transfer when each heading corresponds to evidence the reader can locate; repeated contribution language alone cannot provide that connection.

Visual effectiveness depends on purpose. Topos translates representations and displays proof constructions (pp2,7), while VisualQuality-R1 and Cluster-GT expose computational structure (p4 each). The neural-collapse control progressively refines its method into a numerical assignment example (p6). SARDet's gap-and-bridge diagrams explain motivation (pp5-6), but their unscaled distributions should remain illustrations. Comparable axes and consistent encodings support comparisons in CAN (p8) and R2-SortnRegress (p7). Peer-review assignment organizes assumptions into a systematic twenty-panel grid (p9), trading breadth for small labels and demanding lookup. A caption should state the comparison, encoding, uncertainty, and exception; omitted uncertainty definitions, crowded legends, and stale references interrupt otherwise coherent arguments.

Qualification is also a presentation strength. Hawthorne reports a null judge-label result before changing its behavioral measurement, yet some labels outrun what the displayed traces establish. Goldilocks acknowledges opaque training details (p10). Peer-review assignment distinguishes self-reported expertise from review quality (p10). The R2 control pairs synthetic gains with weak real-data results (p8); the neural-collapse control reports mixed feature-mask effects (p9). These controls counter any category-based ranking of exposition. Transfer the habit of pairing claims with conditions and counterexamples. Do not transfer numerical rankings across incompatible protocols, infer mechanisms from selected examples, or treat readable mathematics as verified proof.
