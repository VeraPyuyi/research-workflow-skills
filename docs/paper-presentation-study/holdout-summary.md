# Held-Out Applicability Check

Thirty held-out papers were assessed against the unchanged frozen cards.
These are qualitative applicability codes, not quality or acceptance scores.

| Card | Fits | Qualified | Not applicable | Counterexample |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| P1 | 24 | 5 | 1 | 0 |
| P2 | 3 | 11 | 16 | 0 |
| P3 | 1 | 20 | 9 | 0 |

| P5 | 24 | 6 | 0 | 0 |
| P6 | 12 | 16 | 0 | 2 |
| P7 | 5 | 15 | 10 | 0 |
| P8 | 12 | 11 | 0 | 7 |

A qualified match retains a limitation; not-applicable is a valid genre/evidence
boundary. Counts do not estimate population frequencies. Counterexamples and
their PDF locators are retained in the detailed project record. No frozen card
was rewritten to improve these counts.

One reader assessed each paper. Contexts were fresh; access boundaries were
instructions on a shared filesystem, not an enforced file jail. Proofs,
experimental correctness and scientific novelty were not certified.
