# Paper expression and visualization study: frozen protocol

Approved on 2026-10-26: 90 papers, five venues, latest three publicly verifiable
editions per venue, four officially selected papers and two ordinary accepted
comparators per venue/year. Retain the original selection label. A presentation
category and an award are not interchangeable outcome variables.

## Sampling and scope

Selection uses official metadata, topical/type tags and a fixed SHA-256 title
ordering seeded by `paper-style-20261026`, before qualitative assessment.
Selected slots seek theory, method, benchmark and empirical perspectives where
available. These title-based tags are sampling aids, not validated classifications;
readers correct genre labels without replacing a paper because of its writing.
Comparators favor related domains and research types; record imperfect matches.
This purposive descriptive study does not estimate a causal effect of writing
style on selection or acceptance and does not represent all conference papers.

Before the reading split is finalized, check title/PDF identity, official
designation, accessible full text, duplicate studies and prior pilot exposure.
For each slot, try at most six ranked candidates when access fails, logging the
attempts. If an edition cannot supply the full cell through that bounded process,
retain the access limitation and use the next older edition. Access failure is
not evidence that a paper or entire year's proceedings do not exist.

Each cell contributes three selected papers and one control to discovery, plus
one selected paper and one control to holdout. A paper previously read in the
planning pilot cannot be held out: swap its split with an unread selected peer
within the same cell, before qualitative discovery starts. Preserve any
preliminary availability manifest and the split-change reason.

## Reading and record

Read the full main text (not just abstract/intro), necessary appendices, and
actual rendered pages containing the figures/tables used in an observation.
Automatically detected body boundaries are provisional; readers must confirm
them. PDF page numbers are the stable locators. Overview thumbnails orient the
reader; detail pages establish visual observations. Record inspected pages,
unread supplements, extraction/visual problems and unavailable material.

For each paper, write an original concise observation record:

- Identity/version, exact official label and its source, PDF digest.
- Main question; argument sequence and section responsibilities with locators.
- Paragraph/language moves and claim-strength handling with locators.
- Figure/table purpose, encoding, caption/body split, layout/readability.
- Transfer conditions, weaknesses/counterexamples, evidence of applicability.
- Actual main-text, visual and relevant appendix coverage.

The record describes presentation. It does not certify the paper's mathematical
proofs, experiments or novelty. Do not redistribute paper PDFs, raw extracted
full text or copied figure assets in the release.

## Pattern and skill assessment

Distinguish a single observation, a repeated observed pattern and an editorial
recommendation. A cross-venue repeated pattern requires at least three independent
papers from two venues, with actual section/page/figure locators. Narrower
patterns state their scope and denominator. Record counterexamples and when a
pattern should not be applied. Freeze pattern cards before inspecting holdouts.

The 30 held-out papers assess transfer and coverage, not acceptance prediction.
Task behavior is assessed separately with the fixed synthetic fixtures and rubric
in tests/paper-presentation-cases.json and tests/paper-presentation-rubric.json.
Old-version outputs must precede instruction changes. New-context executors see
tasks, skills and raw inputs, not the desired answers or baseline assessment.

Preserve factual/numerical/mathematical meaning, negative results, uncertainty,
topic focus and submission privacy. Require one demonstrated incremental benefit
per target skill; otherwise retain the corresponding advice as optional.
Consolidate verification and independent review at the final delivery milestone.
