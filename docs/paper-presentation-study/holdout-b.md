Selected and control papers both exhibit useful diagnostic chains and weaknesses. Genre matters: the ICML position paper develops non-identifiability cases and an agenda, whereas benchmark, mechanistic, compression and theory papers carry different evidential obligations. A not-applicable verdict preserves those distinctions; fits records observed applicability, not writing quality.

P1 and P5 transfer most naturally when a paper connects a motivating discrepancy to a design and then returns to that discrepancy in diagnostics. Reward alignment, compression, PRESTO and COCOA provide different versions of this chain. Ablations organize an argument without automatically identifying causes. COCOA also illustrates P2's distinction between exact coefficients, restricted theoretical results and learned-model behavior. Neural-Q-Whittle's roadmap clarifies proof dependencies, but its finite-width residual must accompany the headline convergence rate. Formal notation alone is insufficient reason to impose a theorem-centered structure on a dataset paper.

P3 and P4 expose important differences between operational definitions and validity. QualiSpeech defines useful dimensions, yet localization metrics condition on successful detection. CHECKWHY labels are argument-relative; they need not certify world truth. H-Test's human rule-identification exercise differs from the models' item-classification task. CLUES uses synthetic corruption and same-source anchors, and its clean-data comparator is surpassed despite upper-bound terminology. PRESTO's expanded scored set contains shared labels, not fourteen times as many independent black-box evaluations. Such denominators, comparison regimes and evaluator boundaries belong near their claims.

P6 is strongest where unfavorable regimes remain consequential: stochastic reward teachers, beneficial context, absent reasoning gains, generation failures and reward aliasing narrow the surviving claims. P8 is empirically useful as a warning. Actual page views revealed caption/value mismatches, changing heatmap ranges, nonzero bar baselines, miniature legends and missing uncertainty. Captions often decode a figure while the body explains its consequence, but local omissions can still mislead. These observations favor checking the exact display supporting a claim over treating visual polish as scientific validation.
