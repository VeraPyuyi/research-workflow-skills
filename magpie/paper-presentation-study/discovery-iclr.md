Operational questions make the presentation assessable. InfoTok moves from an explicit question to formal limitations and a practical compression mechanism (iclr-2026-1, Sections 1-3, pp.1-6). The in-context-learning control defines three generalization directions before testing function combinations (iclr-2025-5, Sections 2-3, pp.2-8); its Figure 1, p.3, turns abstract distinctions into task boxes and arrows. Symbolic Graphics Programs defines a measurable component of understanding and later supplies a difficult digit counterexample (iclr-2025-2, Sections 1/7, pp.1/10-11). These devices transfer when the operational test and its limits remain explicit.

Theory becomes easier to follow when equations have an explanatory bridge. Near-Optimal Coordination uses interpretive Remarks between bounds and algorithms (iclr-2025-1, Sections 3-4, pp.4-9). The causal-discovery control layers assumptions, recovery results and three method stages (iclr-2026-5, Sections 3-4, pp.3-7). The final theory control translates notation into fixed-future-policy intuition and explicitly limits comparisons to upper bounds (iclr-2024-5, Sections 2/3.3, pp.5/7). None of these observations assesses proof validity.

Evaluation papers benefit from showing why their measurements deserve interpretation. FLASK validates human/model agreement before wider capability comparisons (iclr-2024-3, Sections 4-5, pp.5-9). CounselBench connects expert-observed failures to targeted adversarial probes (iclr-2026-3, Sections 4-5, pp.4-10). TRON repeatedly separates marginal coverage from individual-case reliability (iclr-2025-3, Sections 3-4, pp.4-9). These are useful scope controls; agreement is not universal alignment, QA ratings are not therapeutic efficacy, and marginal guarantees are not per-case guarantees.

Inspected visuals often divide explanatory labor: diagrams identify mechanisms, while tables or curves compare outcomes. Examples include PGP's forward/backward flow and grouped accuracy/forgetting table (iclr-2024-2, Figure 2 p.4; Table 1 p.7), and adversarial RL's gait snapshots and robustness heatmaps (iclr-2024-1, Figure 4 p.9; Figure 2 p.8). Captions commonly decode encodings while body text supplies interpretation. Recurring weaknesses include tiny annotations, similar-colored curves, dense matrices and missing local uncertainty definitions. PGP's replacement-glyph figure was inspected directly; fine SVD subscripts remain unreadable and were not inferred.
