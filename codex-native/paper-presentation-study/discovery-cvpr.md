# CVPR discovery synthesis

This analysis covers exactly 12 discovery papers: nine selected papers and three ordinary controls. Selection labels are sampling metadata, not evidence of better writing. This is presentation analysis, not proof, novelty, or reproduction certification. The >=3-papers/2-venues rule is inapplicable within this single-venue subset; these observations do not establish cross-venue patterns. Locators below use PDF pages; exact source URLs and digests remain in `discovery-cvpr.json`.

## Observations

- **Argument continuity appears in controls too.** Robust Audio-Visual Segmentation connects two introductory failures to two modules and returns to them in ablations (cvpr-2025-5, Section 1, pp.1-2; Section 4.4, pp.7-8). CARE builds design rationale from diagnosis (cvpr-2026-5, Sections 2-4, pp.2-8). ODG-CLIP connects a consistency hypothesis to a diagnostic comparison (cvpr-2024-5, Section 4.2, p.7). MDS-VQA makes a useful additional distinction between discovering failures and demonstrating downstream usefulness (cvpr-2026-3, Section 4.2, p.6).

- **Figures make abstract operations inspectable.** Anatomica's branching geometry/topology diagram gives its operators a shared visual grammar (cvpr-2026-1, Fig.2, p.3). GardenDesigner's agent columns show intermediate outputs (cvpr-2026-2, Fig.2, p.4). EgoThink grounds its taxonomy in photographs and questions (cvpr-2024-3, Fig.2, p.3); Q-Eval makes prompt decomposition concrete with a worked example (cvpr-2025-3, Fig.6, p.7). Captions identify components while the body supplies definitions or equations. Tiny prompts, photographs, and inset labels constrain independent inspection.

- **Comparison conditions improve interpretability.** GGEUR separates backbone and augmentation increments (cvpr-2025-1, Table 4, p.7). EgoLM exposes modality availability (cvpr-2025-2, Tables 2-3, p.6). PromptSG pairs an architectural inference choice with relative speed and accuracy (cvpr-2024-1, Fig.3, p.3; Table 5, p.7). CFPL-FAS separates supplementary-data conditions and explains target shots (cvpr-2024-2, Table 2, p.6). These structures help readers see what changes between rows.

- **Local exceptions qualify broad language.** Anatomica retains unimproved void counts (p.8); MDS-VQA's best mean includes dataset-level losses (Table 3, p.6); EgoLM retains specialist wins (Table 2, p.6). CFPL-FAS acknowledges visual-only CLIP-V advantages (Section 4.1, p.6). EgoThink labels its scaling explanation a hypothesis, while its human-agreement check covers only object/planning responses from GPT-4V (Sections 5.1-5.2, p.8). These are counterexamples to universal superiority or evaluator-reliability readings.

- **Visual emphasis can conflict with comparison.** CARE bolds non-minimal safety scores despite its stated convention (Table 1, p.7); CFPL-FAS bolds a lower TPR than competing entries (Table 2, p.6). EgoThink and ODG-CLIP use nonzero bar baselines (Fig.8, p.8; Fig.3, p.7). GardenDesigner's stacked preferences have moving segment baselines (Fig.6, p.8), and the segmentation control's uncertainty heatmaps lack a numeric scale (Fig.6, p.8). These weaknesses cross selection labels.

## Editorial recommendations

Treat these as conditional suggestions, not validated quality rules. Map failures to components and tests when the failure modes are separable; benchmark papers may benefit more from taxonomy-to-example alignment. Keep comparison conditions adjacent to results, label relative units, and reconcile boldface with metric direction. Use captions for decoding and the body for interpretation, with sufficient figure scale to inspect the evidence. State exceptions beside headline gains. Transfer requires checking domain assumptions: GardenDesigner changes rules across garden traditions (Section 5.5, p.8), ODG-CLIP filters uninformative synthetic unknowns (Section 3.1, p.4), and Q-Eval acknowledges temporal information loss (Section 5.2, p.8).

## Coverage and limitations

All 12 complete main-text files were read: 108 extracted pages, pp.1-9 each; substantive arguments occupy pp.1-8, with references beginning on p.9. All 12 overviews and 27 detail pages were viewed. No appendices were read; external supplements remain uninspected. Reference tails received boundary checks, not full reading. All PDF digests match the manifest. ODG-CLIP's comma/apostrophe title difference and CARE's catalog 25-page versus actual 10-page discrepancy are recorded. Q-Eval's corrupted labels were checked in detail images; CARE's fine Fig.3 annotations remain unresolved. Extraction order/markup limitations are recorded per paper; missing visual text was not inferred.
